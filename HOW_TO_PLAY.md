# ECHO — How to Play

Welcome to ECHO. You are trapped in a mysterious facility. The goal is simple: survive and find the exit through 3 increasingly difficult levels.

But there is a catch. **Gemma 4**, a highly intelligent AI, is watching your every move and will actively change the environment to kill you based on your habits.

## 🎮 Controls

ECHO is played entirely with your keyboard. 

- **W / Arrow Up**: Move Up
- **S / Arrow Down**: Move Down
- **A / Arrow Left**: Move Left
- **D / Arrow Right**: Move Right

## 👁️ The Visual Grid

You are represented on the grid as the **glowing white Crosshair**. 

Move onto the following tiles to interact with them:

* 🟥 **Red Skulls (Enemies)**: Move onto them to attack. They will hit back!
* 🟩 **Green Hearts/Keys (Items)**: Move onto them to collect them and heal or gain access to locked areas.
* 🟧 **Orange Octagons (Hazards)**: Watch out! These are hidden traps spawned by the AI. Stepping on them deals heavy damage.
* ⬜ **Gray Doors**: These are exits to other rooms. Moving onto one will teleport you to a new area. Be careful, some doors are locked (dashed lines) and require a Key.

## 🧠 The AI Director

As you play, the panel on the right side of your screen (**ECHO // WORLD INTELLIGENCE**) builds a psychological profile on you. It tracks your aggression and habits.

Every few moves, Gemma will analyze this profile. 
When you see the screen blur with **GEMMA IS ANALYZING YOUR STRATEGY**, the AI is deciding how to mess with you.

A massive red banner will drop down announcing the AI's action. For example:
> **CREATE HAZARD**
> *"You fight recklessly. Let us see how you handle traps."*

The environment will instantly change. New enemies might appear, safe routes might become dangerous, or the floor might fill with traps. You must constantly change your strategy so the AI cannot predict you!

## 🏆 Winning the Game

To win, you must find the Exit Door in the final room of the facility. Once you do, you advance to the next Level. Survive all **3 Levels** to escape and beat the AI.
